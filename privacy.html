Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This is a general starting template, not a document prepared or reviewed by a lawyer. Please have it checked against Nigeria's Data Protection Act before relying on it.

1. Who we are

Melanin Accessories ("we", "us") is an accessories brand based in Ibadan, Nigeria. This policy explains what information this website collects and how it is used.

2. Your wishlist stays in your browser

When you add a product to your wishlist, that list is saved only on your own device, in your browser's local storage. It is never sent to us or stored on our servers unless you choose to send it.

3. Information you choose to share

When you fill in your name and location and tap "Send wishlist on WhatsApp", that information, along with your wishlist items, is placed into a WhatsApp message that you send to us yourself. We only receive it if you choose to send it, the same as sending us any other WhatsApp message.

4. How we use that information

We use the details you send us only to discuss and fulfil your order — confirming availability, arranging payment, and delivery. We do not sell or share your information with third parties.

5. Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may collect limited technical data (such as your IP address) when those fonts load.

6. Your rights

You can clear your wishlist at any time by clearing your browser's site data. For anything you've sent us directly on WhatsApp, you can ask us to delete it by contacting us.

7. Contact us

For any question about this policy, message us on WhatsApp or call +234 815 828 9001.
